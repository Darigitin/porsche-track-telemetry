How downshifting to 3rd gear instead of holding 4th saved 0.300 seconds through Turn 4–5 at High Plains Raceway.

Comparing July vs. October 10Hz CAN-bus telemetry in my 6-speed manual 2023 Porsche 718 Cayman GT4:
• 4th Gear (July): 4,477 apex RPM | +0.20G exit pull | 79.1 MPH into Turn 5
• 3rd Gear (October): 5,411 apex RPM | +0.31G exit pull | 84.7 MPH into Turn 5 (+5.6 MPH)
• Time Gained: 0.300 seconds in a single complex from engine braking rotation and keeping the 4.0L flat-6 in its peak torque window up the hill.
