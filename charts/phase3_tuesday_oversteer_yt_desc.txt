How progressive rear slip helps rotate the mid-engine 718 Cayman GT4 at High Plains Raceway.

Looking at 10Hz CAN-bus telemetry from Saturday Session 1:
• CAN Oversteer Channel (wpoOversteer): 0.24 peak rotation at 70.6 MPH pulling 1.08G lateral load.
• Tire & Chassis Setup: Vitour Tempesta P1s (265/35R19 F, 295/30R19 R at 30.5/31.0 PSI hot) paired with Tarett rear toe links and bump-steer locking plates. Eliminating rubber bushing compliance makes rear slip completely linear and predictable at the limit.
