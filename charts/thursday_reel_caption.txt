How Turn 3 exit momentum unlocked +20.6 MPH down the back straight 🏁⏱️

Top speed at the end of a long straight isn't about horsepower—it's almost entirely determined by minimum apex speed and how early you can unwind the wheel to commit to Wide Open Throttle (WOT).

Looking at my 10Hz CAN telemetry exiting Turn 3 (The Carousel) onto the 2,838-foot back straight at High Plains Raceway:
• WOT Commitment: Increased from 2.2% of the lap in my June 2024 baseline up to 36.6% of the lap.
• The Technique: Taking the Turn 3 apex curbing, letting the 718 GT4 settle onto the 295/30R19 rear Vitour Tempesta P1s (31.0 PSI hot), and squeezing smoothly to 100% throttle as I unwind steering lock toward track-out.
• The Result: Back-straight terminal velocity before the Turn 4 braking zone climbed from 114.2 MPH to 134.8 MPH (+20.6 MPH)—gaining massive time before ever touching the brakes.
