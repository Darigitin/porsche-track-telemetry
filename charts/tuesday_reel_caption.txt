How trailing off the brake pedal cured factory 718 understeer 🏁⏱️

Mid-engine platforms like the 718 Cayman GT4 hate abrupt brake releases. If you snap off the brake pedal before turn-in, the front end unweights, and you get that classic mid-corner push.

Looking at my 10Hz CAN telemetry through Turn 4 (Biker's Berm) at High Plains Raceway:
• Threshold spike: -1.25G peak deceleration in a straight line.
• The Technique: Downshifting to 3rd gear under braking and bleeding off pedal pressure progressively, holding 10–15% trail brake pressure all the way down to the apex curb.
• The Result: Kept the front tires loaded, rotated the rear predictably, and carried 84.7 MPH uphill into Turn 5—saving exactly 0.300 seconds through this complex alone.
