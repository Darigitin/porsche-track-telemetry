Managing vertical load from a compression bowl over a blind crest in the 718 Cayman GT4 🏁📐

Turns 6 and 7 at High Plains Raceway ("Danny's Lesson" up into "High Plains Drifter") are a masterclass in managing vertical tire load:

• Turn 6 (Bottom of the Bowl): You're braking downhill into heavy compression where the tires are pressed into the pavement, giving you huge front-end bite to rotate the car at 36.3 MPH.
• Turn 7 (The Blind Crest): Seconds later, you're accelerating uphill at 96.5 MPH over a completely blind crest where vertical load drops off and the chassis goes light.
• The Setup Difference: If you still have steering angle dialed in as the car crests Turn 7, the rear wants to step out as the tires unload. Unwinding the hands right before the crest—paired with Tarett rear toe links and bump-steer locking plates keeping the rear track dead straight over the rise—lets you stay flat on the throttle all the way into the back S-curves.
