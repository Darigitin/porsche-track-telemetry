Wrapping up the 2026 track season with an off-season chassis & brake teardown checklist 🏁🔧

After 61 sessions and 519 timed laps at High Plains Raceway getting down to a 1:56.55 PB, the 718 Cayman GT4 is going into the garage for a full post-season inspection before next spring:

1️⃣ Brake System & Caliper Puck Inspection:
Flushing with fresh Castrol React SRF (518°F wet boiling point) and swapping in a fresh set of Pagid 4925-RSL29 endurance pads over the 380mm GiroDisc 2-piece floating rotors. While the calipers are off on the Tarett studs, the #1 priority is inspecting the factory Brembo ceramic piston pucks for hairline thermal cracking.

2️⃣ Caliper Thermal Mitigation:
Instead of dropping $2k+ on boutique titanium pistons, adding 1mm GiroDisc titanium pad heat shields (TS-1741) or AutoQuest / Demon Speed replacement pucks drastically drops thermal transfer into the pistons, dust boots, and fluid during 20-minute summer sessions at 5,000+ ft altitude.

3️⃣ Monoball & Alignment Benchmark:
Checking all Tarett spherical bearings, rear toe bump-steer locking plates, and zero-preload drop links to verify nothing shifted after a full season of curb strikes.
