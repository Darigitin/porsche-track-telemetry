Using progressive rear slip to rotate a mid-engine 718 Cayman GT4 at the apex 🏁⏱️

One of my favorite traits of the Vitour Tempesta P1 (265/35R19 front, 295/30R19 rear) is how predictably it communicates at the limit of adhesion. When hot pressures are dialed to 30.5 PSI front / 31.0 PSI rear, the compound doesn't snap—it gives a progressive, controllable rotation that helps point the nose toward corner exit earlier.

Looking at my 10Hz CAN telemetry from Saturday Session 1 at High Plains Raceway:
• CAN Oversteer Channel (wpoOversteer): Spikes to 0.24 mid-corner while pulling 1.08G lateral load at 70.6 MPH.
• Chassis Stability: With Tarett rear toe control arms and bump-steer locking plates eliminating dynamic toe deflection, the rear steps out linearly and catches cleanly with a quick wrist correction without upsetting platform balance.
