Engineering & elevation guide to High Plains Raceway (Full Course — 2.55 miles, 15 turns) in the 718 Cayman GT4 🏁📐

What makes HPR such a technical driver's track is the 300+ feet of elevation change—nearly every major braking and turn-in zone happens over a blind crest or into a heavy compression bowl.

Swipe through for the sector speed map, vertical elevation profile, and the 3 complexes where lap time is won or lost:

1️⃣ Turns 4–5 (Biker's Berm to Niagara Plunge): Braking at -1.25G from 134.8 MPH, downshifting immediately to 3rd gear (5,411 apex RPM), and trailing 15% brake pressure to the curb saves 0.300s pulling up the hill.
2️⃣ Turns 6–7 (Danny's Lesson to High Plains Drifter): Using the compression at the bottom of the bowl to rotate, then unwinding the wheel *before* cresting T7 as the chassis goes light.
3️⃣ Turns 13–15 (The Prairie Corkscrew): Leaning on the downhill compression at 81 MPH to pull 1.54G peak lateral load on 30.5/31.0 PSI hot Vitour Tempesta P1s.
