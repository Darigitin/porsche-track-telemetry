How trailing off the brake pedal cured factory 718 understeer at High Plains Raceway.

Looking at 10Hz CAN-bus telemetry through Turn 4 (Biker's Berm):
• Threshold spike: -1.25G peak deceleration in a straight line.
• The Technique: Downshifting to 3rd gear under braking and bleeding off pedal pressure progressively, holding 10–15% trail brake pressure all the way down to the apex curb.
• The Result: Kept the front tires loaded, rotated the rear predictably, and carried 84.7 MPH uphill into Turn 5—saving 0.300s through this complex alone.
