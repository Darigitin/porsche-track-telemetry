How vertical load changes from the Turn 6 compression bowl over the Turn 7 blind crest at High Plains Raceway in my 2023 Porsche 718 Cayman GT4.

Looking at 10Hz CAN-bus telemetry:
• Turn 6 Compression Bowl: Heavy vertical compression loads the front axle to rotate at the 36.3 MPH apex.
• Turn 7 Blind Crest: Accelerating uphill to 96.5 MPH over the blind rise—unwinding steering lock before the chassis goes light at the crest is critical to keeping the mid-engine platform planted.
