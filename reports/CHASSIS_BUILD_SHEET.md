### 🔍 Caliper Thermal Management & Piston Puck Evaluation
Factory 718 GT4 Brembo calipers use ceramic pucks on the piston faces. Under sustained track heat, these pucks commonly chip, crack, or disintegrate. Porsche does not sell pucks separately (requiring full caliper replacement). 

Instead of aesthetic/boutique brands charging $2k+ for full titanium pistons (e.g. Goldleaf at €2,085), track-proven functional motorsport solutions include:
1. **Demon Speed Motorsports (DSM) Pucks ($108 – $216):** Cup car and club-racing specialists. Direct-replacement pucks in high-temp proprietary composite or Grade 5 Titanium with hardware.
2. **AutoQuest Motorsports Titanium Pucks ($695 Front / $1,165 F+R):** The Rennlist benchmark fix. CNC Grade 5 Titanium pucks bolted directly into existing OEM pistons.
3. **Girodisc Titanium Pad Heat Shields ($150 Front / $120 Rear, `TS-1741-4` / `TS-1740-4`):** 1.0mm Ti-6Al-4V water-jet cut shims placed between pad backing plate and pistons. Acts as an external thermal barrier to protect OEM pucks, dust boots, and brake fluid without tearing down calipers.
4. **Action Plan with Honest Assembly:** Inspect pulled Pagid pads and factory ceramic pucks. If pucks are undamaged, run Girodisc Ti pad shields. If pucks show chipping/cracking, replace pucks with Demon Speed or AutoQuest.
