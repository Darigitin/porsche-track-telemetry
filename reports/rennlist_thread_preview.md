**[SIZE=4]⚙️ Key Chassis & Driving Takeaways[/SIZE]**

**1. The Tire Pressure Sweet Spot on Vitour Tempesta P1 (~30.5 PSI Hot Target)**
• **Cold Start:** 23.5 - 24.0 PSI cold.
• **The Trap:** Starting above 28-30 PSI cold pushed the P1s past 36-38 PSI hot within a few laps, causing the compound to overheat and introducing sharp mid-corner understeer.
• **The Discipline:** Coming in immediately after Session 1 and bleeding down to **30.5 PSI hot front / 31.0 PSI hot rear** kept the tire right in its prime grip window—delivering **1.35G sustained** and peaking at **1.54G** through the Prairie Corkscrew (Turns 13-15).

**2. The Turn 4 Revelation (Braking in 3rd vs 4th Gear: Gaining 0.300s)**
• I compared my July telemetry against my October telemetry through Turn 4 (Biker's Berm) and Turn 5 (Niagara Plunge).
• In July, I held 4th gear through the T4 braking zone. In October, I executed an immediate downshift to 3rd gear during threshold braking at 1,364m.
• **Data Result:**
   - Apex RPM jumped from 4,477 RPM (4th gear) to **5,411 RPM (3rd gear)**.
   - Corner exit acceleration climbed from +0.20G to **+0.31G**.
   - Uphill entry speed into T5 climbed from 79.1 MPH to **84.7 MPH**.
   - **Total time saved across T4-T5: Exactly 0.300 seconds** purely from gear selection and engine braking stability.

**3. Trail-Braking Front Axle Loading**
• The 718 GT4 mid-engine chassis rotates beautifully if I keep 10-15% trail-braking pressure on the nose all the way down to the apex curb.
• Bleeding off the brake pedal smoothly rather than snapping off eliminated my mid-corner push in Turns 8 and 10 completely.
